# Account Settings

The **Settings** page allows you to manage profile information, update your password, or permanently delete your account.

---

## Profile Information

- **Full Name**: Update your display name.
- **Username**: Update your unique username handle (which updates the URL for your mock endpoints).
- **Email Address**: Read-only field verified during registration.

Click **Save Changes** to apply profile updates.

---

## Changing Password

1. Enter your **Current Password**.
2. Enter your **New Password** (minimum 6 characters).
3. Re-enter the new password in the **Confirm New Password** field.
4. Click **Update Password**.

---

## Account Deletion

In the **Danger Zone**, click **Delete Account** to permanently remove your account:
- Erases your user profile and login credentials.
- Deletes all saved APIs, workspaces, and mock endpoints.
- External calls to your mock URLs will return `404 Not Found`.
