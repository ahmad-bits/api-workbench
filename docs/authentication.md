# Authentication

API Workbench uses an account system with email verification (OTP), flexible sign-in, and password recovery.

---

## Sign Up & Email Verification

1. Click **Sign Up** from the navigation.
2. Enter your Name, Username, Email, and Password.
3. Click **Continue** to receive a 6-digit verification code via email.
4. Enter the code to activate your account and log in.

---

## Login

1. Click **Login** from the navigation.
2. Enter your **Username or Email** and your **Password**.
3. Click **Sign In**.

---

## Password Reset

1. Click **Forgot password?** on the login screen.
2. Enter your registered email or username and click **Send Reset Code**.
3. Enter the 6-digit verification code sent to your email and click **Verify Code**.
4. Enter and confirm your new password, then click **Reset Password**.
